PA2 TIM5_CH3 supplies the lamp PWM, with a 16-bit duty register. PB8/PB9 supply
the MCP4728 bus. LCD1 A/B and LCD2 C/D have separately programmable envelopes.
Both electrodes reverse around a 1.5 V common mode at nominal 100 Hz. Each full
AC pair uses a latched amplitude; UDAC staging and software update commit pairs.
The same-address two-board arrangement is intentional and documented in polarizer.

Finite waveform buffers contain 2..257 points. Requests are bounded to five seconds.
The normal lamp command ceiling is 6 V nominal RMS. Explicit high mode permits
brief commands up to 9 V, with a cumulative above-6-V time budget of 180 ms.
Normal cooldown is three seconds; high mode uses five seconds. The source is a
6 V-rated bulb; a conservative 12.5 V supply bound is used for duty calculation.
These are nominal PWM equivalent voltages, not direct lamp-voltage measurements.
I2C fault, cancel and completion force lamp duty zero. A watchdog is enabled.

The pilot worked. A later host disk-writer queue overflow aborted the partial main
run. The final reported hardware state was busy=0, duty=0, lamp_mv=0, both LCD
amplitudes=0, fault=0 and ack_failures=0. The host throughput fix needs no new flash.
