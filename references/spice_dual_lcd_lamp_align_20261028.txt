Dual-LCD plus lamp alignment cycle, 2026-10-28
===========================================

User confirmed that both bare LCDs visibly tune in the preserved DLC2
diagnostic, then requested indefinite lamp cycling at nominal 4 V:
5 seconds illuminated, 3 seconds cooling, while adjusting optical angles.

This is a new separate DLA1 image. The DLC2 source, build and controls are
unchanged. Actual previous flash and its ELF/BIN are preserved locally under
backups/dual_lcd_lamp_align_<timestamp> before flashing the new image.
The original earlier SPICE acquisition flash backup is also unchanged.

New source:
firmware/stm32_sensor_head_lcd/src/spice_dual_lcd_lamp_align_main.c
New ELF:
firmware/stm32_sensor_head_lcd/build_spice_dual_lcd_lamp_align/stm32_spice_dual_lcd_lamp_align.elf
Identity: dual_magic=0x444C4131 (DLA1).

Build with the existing Makefile, without changing that Makefile:
make -C firmware/stm32_sensor_head_lcd PROJECT=stm32_spice_dual_lcd_lamp_align BUILD_DIR=build_spice_dual_lcd_lamp_align APP_SRCS=src/spice_dual_lcd_lamp_align_main.c -j8

Wiring stays as confirmed:
PB8=SCL, PB9=SDA, 3.3 V DAC VCC/LDAC, common ground.
One unique DAC address is currently 0x60; boards mirror four-channel writes.
LCD1 is on A/B, LCD2 is on C/D, retaining series resistors.
PA2/TIM5_CH3 is the tungsten MOS PWM. PA0/PA1/PA3/PA4/PA5 stay low.

Both LCD envelopes retain the preceding opposite 1 s rise / 1 s fall scan,
with balanced 100 Hz carrier, independent A/B and C/D differential drives,
and amplitudes summing to 3000 mV. Optical transmissions need not sum flat.

The lamp uses the proven PSC=6, ARR=65535 TIM5 carrier (about 443 Hz for
the current clock), CCR3 duty=6710/65536 during illumination, zero otherwise.
This follows duty=(4.0/12.5)^2 with integer truncation. It represents about
4 V RMS only under the saved supply assumption, not steady 4 V DC or a
direct voltage measurement. A 12 V actual supply would give about 3.84 V
ideal PWM RMS. Supply changes require reassessing this command.

Firmware clamps on-time to at most 5000 ms, cooldown to at least 3000 ms,
and the nominal target to at most 4000 mV. Timing is on the MCU, not host
sleeps. Lamp cycling defaults disarmed on boot/reset and is explicitly
armed by the user's alignment command. A stalled main loop is watchdog-
reset and the lamp is disarmed afterward. This is not a certified thermal
interlock. I2C faults or disabled LCD operation also force lamp duty zero.

Control from stm32dev:
python scripts/spice_dual_lcd_lamp_align_control.py align --confirm-lcd2-on-cd --rise-ms 1000
python scripts/spice_dual_lcd_lamp_align_control.py status
python scripts/spice_dual_lcd_lamp_align_control.py lamp-stop
python scripts/spice_dual_lcd_lamp_align_control.py stop

lamp-stop keeps the LCD sweeps; stop disables both LCD and lamp drive.
The explicit alignment loop intentionally remains active after the host
process exits, until stopped or reset. The old DLC2/SEF controllers have
different identity guards and must not write the alignment image.

Status reads TIM5 CCR3/ARR rather than PA2 ODR to determine the PWM command:
an alternate-function PWM pin's ODR is not its active waveform.
No camera acquisition, spectral calibration, or automatic angle finding
is performed for this visual-alignment request.
